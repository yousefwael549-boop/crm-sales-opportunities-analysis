## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing CRM sales opportunities to evaluate revenue performance, understand sales pipeline effectiveness, and identify opportunities for business growth.

The analysis was conducted using SQL Server for data exploration and business analysis, and Power BI for data modeling, visualization, and interactive reporting.

## 2. Business Problem

Sales managers need a clear understanding of sales performance to make informed business decisions.

This project addresses key business challenges related to:
- Revenue generation and growth.
- Deal conversion and win rates.
- Sales representative effectiveness.
- Product and customer performance.
- Pipeline management and sales cycle efficiency.

## 3. Tools & Technologies

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|

| Power BI | Data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive dashboards |

## 4. Key Business Questions

- How is revenue changing over time?
- Which sales representatives generate the most revenue?
- Which representatives achieve the highest win rates?
- Which products perform best in terms of revenue and deal conversion?
- How are open opportunities distributed across pipeline stages?
- How long does it take to close a deal?
- How does sales performance vary across regions and customer sectors?

## 5. SQL Analysis

SQL Server was used to perform the following analyses:

- Overall sales performance and win rate.
- Monthly and quarterly revenue trends.
- Sales representative performance and ranking.
- Product performance and revenue contribution.
- Account and sector analysis.
- Sales cycle duration analysis.
- Open sales pipeline distribution.

## 6. Power BI Dashboards

The project includes three interactive dashboards:

- **Executive Sales Overview:** Provides an overview of revenue, sales performance, and key business KPIs.
- **Sales Team Performance:** Evaluates sales representatives based on revenue, win rate, and deal performance.
- **Pipeline & Product Analysis:** Examines pipeline distribution, product performance, and sales opportunities.

## 7. Key Findings

### Overall Sales Performance
- The company achieved an overall win rate of **63% in 2017**.

### Regional Performance
- Win rates were relatively similar across regions.
- The East region achieved the highest average deal value.

### Customer Distribution
- **73 out of 85 customers (86%)** are located in the West region, mainly in the US, indicating a significant geographic concentration.

### Sales Representative Performance
- **6 out of the top 10 sales representatives** belong to the West region.

### Product Performance
- GTK500 generated no sales in the East or Central regions, indicating limited regional market penetration.

## 8. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

- **Geographic Diversification:** Expand the customer base beyond the US market to reduce geographic concentration risk.
- **Salesforce Optimization:** Review salesforce allocation based on workload, regional coverage, and revenue contribution.
- **Pipeline Management:** Regularly review aging opportunities to identify potential bottlenecks and improve deal conversion.
- **Product Expansion:** Develop targeted sales strategies to increase GTK500 penetration in the East and Central regions.
