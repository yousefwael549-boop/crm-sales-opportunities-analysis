# Data

This folder contains the raw datasets used in the CRM Sales Opportunities Analysis project.

## Files

- `sales_pipeline.csv`: Sales opportunities, deal stages, revenue, and sales agents.
- `sales_teams.csv`: Sales representatives and team information.
- `products.csv`: Product details.
- `accounts.csv`: Customer account information, sectors, and locations.

## Data Source

[Maven Analytics – CRM Sales Opportunities](https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/crm-sales-opportunities)
