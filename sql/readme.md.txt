CRM Sales Opportunities Analysis

1. Project Overview
An end-to-end Sales Analytics project using SQL Server and Power BI to evaluate sales performance, understand the sales pipeline, and identify opportunities for improving business outcomes.
2. Business Problem
Sales managers need to understand revenue performance, deal conversion, sales representative effectiveness, and product-level performance to make informed decisions.

3. Tools
- SQL Server: Data exploration and business analysis
- Power BI: Data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive dashboards
- Power Query: Data cleaning and transformation
4. Key Business Questions
- How is revenue changing over time?
- Which sales representatives generate the most revenue?
- Which representatives have the highest win rates?
- Which products perform best in revenue and deal conversion?
- How are open opportunities distributed across pipeline stages?
- How long does it take to close a deal?

5. SQL Analysis
- Overall sales performance and win rate
- Monthly and quarterly revenue trends
- Sales representative ranking
- Product and account sector analysis
- Sales cycle analysis
- Open pipeline analysis

6. Power BI Dashboards
- Executive Sales Overview
- Sales Team Performance
- Pipeline & Product Analysis

7. Key Findings and Recommendations
Add findings calculated from the actual dataset, explain their business implications, and recommend practical actions supported by the evidence.
